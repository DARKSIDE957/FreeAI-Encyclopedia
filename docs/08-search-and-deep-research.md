## Detailed Research Tool Breakdowns

### 1. Perplexity AI (The Search Standard)
* **Website**: [perplexity.ai](https://perplexity.ai)
* **Free Capabilities**:
  * **Quick Search**: Completely unlimited queries. Delivers synthesized answers with verified footnote links.
  * **Pro Search**: 5 deep searches every 4 hours. Executes multiple follow-up searches autonomously, reads dozens of pages, and resolves complex questions.
  * **Collections & Library**: Organize search histories into folders and publish research summaries as public web pages.
* **Paid Plan**: $20/month.
* **Is Paying Worth It?**: **6 / 10**  
  * *Verdict*: Excellent for full-time researchers, but casual users will rarely need more than the 5 Pro searches available every 4 hours (which equals 30 deep research queries per day).

---

### 2. Genspark (The Multi-Agent Alternative)
* **Website**: [genspark.ai](https://genspark.ai)
* **Free Capabilities**:
  * Utilizes autonomous AI agents to research different facets of a query in parallel.
  * Generates **"Sparkpages"** — dynamic, interactive web pages complete with structured tables, pros/cons comparisons, and video summaries.
  * Completely free without aggressive paywalls.

---

### 3. Consensus.app (Science & Medical Fact Checking)
* **Website**: [consensus.app](https://consensus.app)
* **What It Does**: Ask any scientific, medical, or sociological question (e.g., *"Does creatine improve cognitive function?"*).
* **Free Capabilities**:
  * Searches over 200 million peer-reviewed scientific studies.
  * Provides a "Consensus Meter" showing the percentage of scientific papers that agree, disagree, or are inconclusive.
  * 20 free synthesized query summaries per month.

---

### 4. Elicit (Automated Literature Reviews)
* **Website**: [elicit.com](https://elicit.com)
* **What It Does**: Analyzes academic PDFs, extracts methodology, sample sizes, outcomes, and caveats into a clean comparative spreadsheet.
* **Free Capabilities**:
  * 5,000 free starter research credits.
  * High-accuracy paper summarization without hallucinated citations.
